Wagering Requirements Explained

Another key aspect of bonuses on PHP777Pro is the wagering requirements attached to them. For example, if a player receives a bonus of RM100 with a wagering requirement of 20x, they must wager RM2,000 (RM100 x 20) before they are eligible to cash out.

Understanding Wagering Requirements

What Are E-Wallets?

E-wallets, or electronic wallets, are digital tools that allow users to store, send, and receive money online. They can be accessed via computers or mobile devices, making them a convenient choice for transactions in the increasingly digital world. Users only need to create an account with their chosen e-wallet provider, link it to their bank account or credit card, and they can start using it to make payments, including those for online casinos. E-wallets work by acting as an intermediary between a user's bank and the recipient. When a player wants to deposit funds into an online casino, the transaction is initiated from the e-wallet app, and the funds are moved almost instantly. This system bypasses the traditional banking mechanisms that often involve multiple layers of approval and processing time.

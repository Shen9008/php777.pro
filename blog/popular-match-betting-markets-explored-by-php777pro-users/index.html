Match Winner

The match-winner market is the most direct form of betting where users select the team they believe will emerge victorious. This market is straightforward and accessible, making it ideal for beginners. For example, if you’re betting on a Malaysian Super League match between KL City FC and Selangor FC, you would simply pick which team you believe is more likely to win.
One of the primary reasons this market remains popular is its simplicity. Bettors don't need elaborate statistical models or deep tactical insights to make a pick; it’s all about their confidence in one team's ability to outperform another.

Handicap Betting

Handicap betting introduces a layer of complexity intended to balance the perceived strength discrepancy between two teams. This market gives a virtual advantage or disadvantage to one team, thus increasing excitement and potential stake amounts.
For example, in a football match where a strong team faces a weaker contender, PHP777Pro may set a handicap of -1.5 goals for the favorite team. If you bet on the favorite, they must win by at least two goals for your bet to be successful.

Handicap betting allows bettors to engage with events characterized by unbalanced odds, stimulating higher risk-reward dynamics. PHP777Pro ensures that all handicap calculations are clear, so users can make informed decisions while taking advantage of the enticing odds available.

Welcome Bonus Red Flags

Avoid offers with 50x or higher wagering – you may never clear them. Watch for short time limits (e.g. 3 days) – not enough time to wager. Check max bet during bonus – often 5 or 10% of bonus; exceeding can void winnings. Verify eligible games – some slots are excluded. Check payment method exclusions – Skrill/Neteller sometimes don't qualify. "No wagering" bonuses are rare but exist – usually with lower match or free spins with capped winnings.

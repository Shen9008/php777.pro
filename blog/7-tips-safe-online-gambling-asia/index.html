Use Strong Passwords & 2FA

Create a unique password for your casino account. Enable two-factor authentication if available. Never share login details with anyone.

5

Stick to Reputable Payment Methods

Know When to Stop

Red Flags – When to Avoid a Casino

No licence or unclear licensing – avoid. Unencrypted site (no padlock) – never enter payment details. Unrealistic bonus promises (e.g. "500% match, no wagering") – usually a scam. No customer support or contact details – red flag. Negative reviews about delayed payouts – proceed with caution. Pressure to deposit more – walk away. A trustworthy casino lets you play at your own pace and never pressure you.

Remember

Gambling can be addictive. Play for fun, set limits, and never chase losses. If you need help, visit BeGambleAware, GamCare, or NCPG.

What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

If a casino refuses a payout, contact support first. Escalate to the licence holder (e.g. Malta Gaming Authority, Curacao) if unresolved. If you suspect fraud, report to your bank and local authorities. If you've developed a gambling problem, use self-exclusion tools and contact organisations like GamCare or BeGambleAware. Document everything – screenshots, emails, transaction IDs – in case you need to dispute a claim.

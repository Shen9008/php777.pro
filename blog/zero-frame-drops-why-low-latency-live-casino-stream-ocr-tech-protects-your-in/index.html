The Importance of Frame Drops in Gaming

Frame drops are a significant concern in live casino environments since they can disrupt a player's experience. A frame drop occurs when the visual feed from the gaming table to the player's device lags, causing a pause or loss of continuity in the gaming experience. In a fast-paced game setting, frame drops can result in delays in player actions, which may lead to missed betting opportunities or hasty decisions.

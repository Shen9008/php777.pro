Markets include match winner, over/under goals, both teams to score, correct score, and handicaps. Asian handicap is especially popular – it removes the draw and gives underdogs a virtual head start. 5 Football Betting Markets Explained

1

Match Winner (1X2)

Home win, draw, or away win. The simplest market. Odds reflect probability.

2

Over/Under Goals

Bet on total goals (e.g. Over 2.5). No need to predict the winner.

3

Both Teams to Score

Yes or no – will both teams score? Popular for high-scoring leagues.

4

Asian Handicap

Level the playing field. E.g. Team A -0.5 means they must win. Removes draw.

5

Correct Score

Predict exact result (e.g. 2-1). Higher odds, harder to hit.

Basketball Betting – NBA, EuroLeague & Asian Leagues

Basketball is huge in Asia. PHP777 covers NBA, EuroLeague, CBA (China), B.League (Japan), and PBA (Philippines). Spread betting (point spread) is the most popular – you bet on whether a team wins by more or less than the spread. E.g. Lakers -5.5 means they must win by 6 or more. Totals (over/under points) and player props (points, rebounds, assists) add depth. Live betting on basketball is thrilling – odds shift quickly as the game progresses. Basketball has many scoring events, so in-play markets update constantly. Player props are ideal if you follow specific stars.

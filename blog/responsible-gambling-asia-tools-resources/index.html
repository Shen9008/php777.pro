7 Responsible Gambling Tools & Tips

1

Set Deposit Limits

Cap how much you can deposit daily, weekly, or monthly. PHP777 offers this in account settings. Stick to your limit.

2

Loss Limits

Some casinos let you set a maximum loss per period. Once reached, you can't deposit until the period resets.

3

Session Reminders

Get alerts after a set time. Helps you stay aware of how long you've been playing.

4

Self-Exclusion

Block yourself from the casino for a period (e.g. 6 months, 1 year, permanent). Use if you need a break.

5

Reality Checks

Pop-up reminders showing time played and balance. Helps you stay grounded.

6

Never Chase Losses

Losing streaks happen. Chasing losses leads to bigger losses. Stop when you've hit your limit.

7

Seek Help

If gambling is affecting your life, reach out. BeGambleAware, GamCare, NCPG offer free, confidential support.

Support Organisations

BeGambleAware – Free advice and support. 24/7 helpline. GamCare – Free support, treatment, and counselling. NCPG – US-based, resources for problem gambling. Gamblers Anonymous – Peer support groups worldwide. GamTalk – Online forum for peer support. These organisations help players worldwide, including in Asia. PHP777 links to these resources on our Responsible Gambling page. Many offer chat, email, and phone support. All are confidential.

Signs of Problem Gambling

Chasing losses – betting more to win back what you lost. Gambling with money meant for bills or essentials. Lying about gambling. Borrowing to gamble. Feeling anxious or irritable when not gambling. Gambling to escape stress or problems. If you recognise these in yourself or someone close to you, reach out. Problem gambling is treatable. Self-exclusion is a powerful first step – it creates a barrier between you and the casino while you seek support.

Remember

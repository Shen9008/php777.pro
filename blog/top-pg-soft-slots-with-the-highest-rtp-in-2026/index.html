What is RTP?

RTP, or Return to Player, is a crucial term in the online gaming industry. It represents the percentage of wagered money that a slot machine is programmed to return to players over time. For instance, if a slot has an RTP of 96%, it means that over the long run, players can expect to get back RM96 for every RM100 they wager. Understanding RTP is vital for players, as it helps them make informed decisions about which games to play and those with better potential for returns.

RTP percentages are calculated over millions of spins and can vary between different casinos and platforms. It’s important to note that while RTP gives a theoretical return rate, individual players may experience outcomes that differ significantly over short periods. Therefore, RTP is one of several factors to consider when choosing a game.

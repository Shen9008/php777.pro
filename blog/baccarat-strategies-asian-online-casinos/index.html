Baccarat Basics

  • Three bets: Player, Banker, Tie
  • Banker has ~1.06% house edge (best)
  • Player has ~1.24% house edge
  • Tie has ~14.4% house edge (avoid)

5 Baccarat Strategies Explained

1

Always Bet Banker

Statistically the best bet. Banker wins slightly more often. Casino takes 5% commission on Banker wins – still the lowest house edge. Simple and effective.

2

Martingale (Double After Loss)

Double your bet after each loss. When you win, you recover losses plus profit. Risk: long losing streaks can exhaust your bankroll. Use with strict limits.

3

Paroli (Double After Win)

Double your bet after each win. Cap at 3–4 wins, then reset. Lets you ride winning streaks without huge risk. More conservative than Martingale.

4

Flat Betting

Same bet every hand. No progression. Preserves bankroll, reduces variance. Boring but safe. Good for long sessions.

5

1-3-2-6 System

Bet sequence: 1, 3, 2, 6 units. After a loss, restart. After 4 wins, you're ahead. Limits exposure compared to Martingale.

Why the Tie Bet is a Trap

The Tie bet pays 8:1 or 9:1 – tempting when you see a run of ties. But the house edge is around 14.4% – far worse than Banker (1.06%) or Player (1.24%). Over time, the Tie bet drains your bankroll. Some players bet Tie "for fun" – fine if you accept the poor odds. For serious play, stick to Banker. The 5% commission on Banker wins is worth it for the better odds.

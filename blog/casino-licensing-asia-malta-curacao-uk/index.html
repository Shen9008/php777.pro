Malta Gaming Authority (MGA)

Malta is one of the strictest licensing jurisdictions. The Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) requires rigorous audits, player protection measures, and anti-money laundering compliance. MGA-licensed casinos must segregate player funds and submit to regular audits. Many top Asian-facing casinos hold Malta licences. Look for the MGA logo in the footer. PHP777 and similar reputable brands often operate under Malta or equivalent licensing.

UK Gambling Commission (UKGC)

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) is considered the gold standard. UK-licensed casinos must meet extremely high standards: responsible gambling tools, fair games, transparent terms. However, UK-licensed sites often restrict players from certain countries – including many in Asia. If you're in Asia, you're more likely to play at Malta or Curacao-licensed sites. Both can be trustworthy when the operator is reputable.

Curacao eGaming

Curacao eGaming is the most common licence for Asian-facing casinos. It's less strict than Malta or UK but still requires basic standards. Many legitimate casinos hold Curacao licences – the key is to choose established brands with good reputations. Curacao-licensed doesn't mean unsafe – but do extra due diligence: check reviews, game providers, and payment options. PHP777 and similar trusted Asian casinos often operate under Curacao. Curacao has been reforming its licensing – newer sublicensees (e.g. Curaçao eGaming) may have stricter requirements than older master licence holders. Look for the specific licence number and verify it if in doubt.

Other Licences – Isle of Man, Gibraltar, Kahnawake

You may also see Isle of Man, Gibraltar, or Kahnawake licences. All are reputable. Isle of Man and Gibraltar are UK-associated and have strong oversight. Kahnawake (Canada) has been licensing online casinos for decades. Any of these is preferable to no licence. If a casino shows no licence or only a vague "licensed" claim without details, be cautious.

5 Ways to Verify a Casino is Licensed

1

Check the Footer

Licence info is usually in the footer. Look for MGA, UKGC, or Curacao logos and licence numbers.

2

Verify on Regulator Site

MGA and UKGC have public registers. Search the casino name to confirm the licence is valid.

3

Read Terms & Conditions

Licensed casinos disclose licensing in T&Cs. Red flag if they hide or omit this.

4

Check Game Providers

Pragmatic Play, Evolution, NetEnt – certified providers. Unlicensed casinos often use unknown software.

5

Player Reviews

Established licensed casinos have reviews and reputation. New, unlicensed sites often have complaints.

What If a Licensed Casino Refuses a Payout?

Contact customer support first. If unresolved, escalate to the regulator. Malta (MGA) and UK (UKGC) have dispute resolution processes. Curacao licensees may have a complaints procedure – check the casino's terms. Document everything: screenshots, emails, transaction IDs. Regulators can pressure casinos to honour valid claims. Unlicensed casinos have no such recourse – another reason to stick with licensed sites.
